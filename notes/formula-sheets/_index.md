# Formula Sheets Index

One sheet per subject. These are what you read in the last 3 weeks before the exam, so they must be **short** (exam version = 1–2 pages) and **yours** (written from your notes and error log, not copied from a coaching PDF).

## Lifecycle

| Stage | When | What |
|-------|------|------|
| Started | During the subject's first pass (Friday PM slot in the phase file) | Formulas + facts as you learn them |
| Complete | Last week of the subject's first pass | Everything a PYQ has needed so far |
| Exam version | Phase 5 revision week (R3) | Trimmed to 1–2 pages: only what you actually forget, plus traps |
| Final read | Phase 6 R4 (Fri PM) + Phase 7 (twice) | Read-only — no additions after Week 70 except new traps |

## Rules

- A formula earns a place on the exam version only if you've **used it in a PYQ** or **got it wrong** at least once.
- Every formula gets its conditions ("only when…", "assumes…") — most GATE formula errors are condition errors.
- Units and rounding conventions go next to NAT-heavy formulas (delays, AMAT, disk time, throughput).
