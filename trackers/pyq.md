## Rules (from `plan/PLAN.md`)

- **Pool:** GATE CS 2000–2021 (pre-2000 only as top-up). **2022+ is sealed** — mocks only.
- **Pass 1 (Phases 1–4):** weekday AM = even years + 2021; Saturday tests = odd years. Together they cover 2000–2021 once.
- **Pass 2 (Phase 5):** every 2000–2021 question again, subject-wise, timed.
- **Counting:** a question counts as "correct" only if right on the first timed attempt with no hint. Redos don't change pass counts — they go in `error-log.md`.
- **Pool size:** in Week 1, count the 2000–2021 questions per subject from the GO PDF / GO subject pages and fill the "Pool" column. Don't guess it.
