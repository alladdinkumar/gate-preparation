# Revision Tracker

Spaced repetition per subject. Forgetting is the silent killer of a 72-week plan: Phase 1 material is ~14 months old by exam day. Updated every Sunday.

---

## Revision Rounds

| Round | When | What it is | Where it's scheduled |
|-------|------|------------|----------------------|
| **R1** | End of the subject's first pass (same or next Saturday) | Re-read all short notes for the subject | Phase files, Saturday 12:00–13:00 |
| **R2** | ~4–12 weeks after R1 | 15-min formula-sheet / notes pass on the Saturday slot | Phase 2–4 files ("R2 revision: …") |
| **Interleaved quiz** | Phase 3–5, every Wednesday PM | 10 timed questions on an earlier subject | Phase 3–5 files |
| **R3** | Phase 5 (Weeks 50–60) | Full second pass: all PYQs again, repair, exam-version formula sheet | `phase-5-revision.md` |
| **R4** | Phase 6 (Weeks 61–70), Friday PM rotation | Exam-version formula sheet + 15 random PYQs | `phase-6-test-series.md` |
| **R5** | Phase 7 (Weeks 71–72) | Formula sheets ×2 + full error-log sweep | `phase-7-final-sprint.md` |

**Stale rule:** any finished subject untouched for **30+ days** → schedule a 1.5h revision slot within the week (coach threshold in `plan/PLAN.md`). Any individual topic untouched for **21+ days** → add a 15-min refresh to a Saturday.
