# Phase 7 — Final Sprint (Weeks 71–72) + Exam Window

**Dates:** 2028-01-17 → 2028-01-30 (2 weeks), then the exam window until your paper
**Budget:** ~38 hours (19h/week), then 30–45 min/day
**Theme:** Consolidate, don't expand. No new topics, no new resources, no new strategies. Walk into the exam rested, calibrated and boring.

---

## Why This Phase Exists

The last 3 weeks before GATE are where many well-prepared candidates lose marks — to panic-learning new material, to sleep debt, to changing a strategy that worked in 12 mocks. This phase exists to prevent that.

Everything you'll use on exam day already exists: 10 exam-version formula sheets, the error log, the exam-day strategy in `exam-info.md`.

**This phase never shrinks** (see `PLAN.md` phase transitions).

---

## Phase Goals

- [ ] Every "not redone correctly" row in `error-log.md` redone

- [ ] Exam logistics fully sorted by Thursday of Week 72

## Exam Window (2028-01-31 → your exam)

Once the structured weeks end, you're in maintenance mode. Your paper date is on the admit card — fill it in `trackers/progress.md`.

### Daily routine (exam window)

| Slot | What |
|------|------|
| Morning, 30–45 min, at exam session clock time | 1–2 formula sheets + 10 PYQs from their error-log rows |
| Rest of day | Work, walk, normal life. No lectures, no new mocks |
| Evening | Read the traps list once (5 min). Sleep on schedule |

### Countdown

| When | What |
|------|------|
| Exam − 7 days | Last full mock allowed (only if the exam is ≥10 days after Week 72 — otherwise skip). Sleep schedule locked |
| Exam − 3 days | No timed tests from here. Formula sheets + traps only |
| Exam − 1 day | Traps list once. Pack: admit card (printed), photo ID, permitted items per brochure, water. Confirm route + reporting time. Early dinner. Screen off 1h before bed |
| Exam day | Wake at schedule. Light breakfast. Reach the centre early (reporting time on the admit card). In the exam: follow the exam-day strategy in `exam-info.md` exactly — GA first, 3 passes, guessing rule |
| Exam + 1 day | Rest. Don't discuss answers online |
| Response sheet / answer key released | Compute your score from the official key, log it in `trackers/mocks.md` as "GATE 2028 (actual)". File challenges only with evidence |

### Logistics Checklist

- [ ] Admit card downloaded and printed (2 copies)
- [ ] Name / photo / category / exam date + session verified on the admit card
- [ ] Photo ID matching the application
- [ ] Exam centre route + travel time checked on the same weekday and time
- [ ] Permitted / prohibited items read from the official brochure
- [ ] Exam session time noted; mocks and sleep aligned to it
- [ ] Leave from work arranged for exam day (and the day before if travel is long)

## After the Exam

1. **Log the actual score** and section-wise estimate in `trackers/mocks.md` and `trackers/progress.md`.
2. **Final retrospective:** `reviews/monthly/2028-02.md` — what worked, what you'd change, whether the PSU / M.Tech path is still the goal.
3. **Results:** check the official results date. When results are out, record score + AIR.
4. **PSU applications:** track notifications that accept GATE 2028 CS scores (per `exam-info.md`). Apply to every eligible one — add each to a table in `trackers/progress.md` with application date and status.
5. **M.Tech fallback:** if your score clears IIT / IISc thresholds, track COAP / individual institute application windows in parallel. Don't choose until offers exist.
6. **Protect rest.** Take a full week off from anything exam-related after the paper. You've earned it.
