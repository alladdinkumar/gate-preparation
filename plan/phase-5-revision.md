# Phase 5 — Revision + PYQ Pass 2 (Weeks 50–60)

**Dates:** 2027-08-23 → 2027-11-07 (11 weeks)
**Budget:** ~209 hours (19h/week)
**Structure:** One subject per week, second full pass. Week 60 ends with the first full-length mock.
**Theme:** Turn "studied once" into "exam-ready". Every 2000–2021 PYQ attempted again, weak topics repaired, formula sheets cut down to exam versions.

---

## Why This Phase Exists

At the end of Phase 4, Phase 1 subjects are ~10 months old. Without a structured second pass, the first-pass accuracy numbers are fiction. This phase has three jobs:

1. **PYQ pass 2** — every 2000–2021 question in the subject, year block by year block. Both even and odd years this time, timed.
2. **Targeted repair** — re-watch *only* the lecture segments for topics with confidence ≤3 or accuracy <60%. Never the whole playlist.
3. **Exam-version formula sheets** — each subject's sheet trimmed to 1–2 pages of what you actually forget.

This is also the **buffer phase**. Any Phase 1–4 extension comes out of here first (11 weeks → minimum 8). If you've used extension weeks, merge the lowest-weight subjects: DL + COA share a week, TOC + CD share a week, and C + DS + Algorithms get 1.5 weeks.

**External dates inside this phase:**
- **GATE 2028 registration** is expected to open around Sep 2027 — check the official site in every Sunday review from Week 50, and register **within the first week it opens**. Don't wait for the deadline.
- Upload-ready documents (photo, signature, category / ID proofs, DigiLocker) prepared in Week 50.

## The Standard Revision Week

Every week follows this skeleton. The per-week tables below fill in the subject, the year blocks and the likely repair topics.

| Slot | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| Mon AM | Redo last Saturday's errors → PYQ pass 2, year block A |
| Mon PM | Read all short notes + formula sheet for the subject (60 min); mark every topic ≤3 confidence (30 min) |
| Tue AM | PYQ pass 2, year block B |
| Tue PM | Targeted repair #1 — re-watch only the segment for the weakest topic; fix the note |
| Wed AM | PYQ pass 2, year block C |
| Wed PM | 30-min interleaved quiz on **last week's** revision subject → targeted repair #2 |
| Thu AM | PYQ pass 2, year block D |
| Thu PM | Re-attempt **every** error-log row ever logged for this subject (all phases) |
| Fri AM | Top-up practice on the 2 weakest topics (pre-2000 PYQs / GO Classes free practice) |
| Fri PM | Trim the formula sheet to its exam version; log R3 in `trackers/revision.md` |
| Sat 09:00–11:00 | 2h full-subject test: 30 questions drawn from 2000–2021 that you got **wrong in pass 1** + random, timed, negative marking |
| Sat 11:00–12:00 | GA — even weeks: rotation topic from your weakest GA area; odd weeks: full mixed GA section (25 min) + analysis |
| Sat 12:00–13:00 | Error analysis, confidence update in `subjects.md`, pass-2 counts in `pyq.md` |
| Sun | Weekly review (+ monthly where marked); check GATE 2028 registration status |

**Year blocks** (default; adjust so each block is ~12–18 questions for the subject): A = 2000–2006 · B = 2007–2012 · C = 2013–2017 · D = 2018–2021.
