## Slot Definitions

### Morning slot (Mon–Fri, 08:30 – 10:00, 1.5h)
**Purpose:** Active recall. PYQs + numericals on yesterday evening's topic. Fresh brain → highest leverage.
**Do:**
- Redo yesterday's error-log rows (10–15 min)
- Solve today's PYQ quota, timed (~3 min per 1-mark, ~5 min per 2-mark) (60 min)
- Log every wrong / guessed question in `trackers/error-log.md` immediately (10 min)
**Don't:**
- Watch lectures
- Read theory before attempting (attempt first, then check the concept)
- Update weekly trackers

**Monday morning exception:** there is no "yesterday's lecture" (Sunday is rest) — Monday AM is either the first lecture of a new subject (when a subject starts) or PYQs on last Friday/Saturday's weakest topic.

### Evening slot (Mon–Fri, 20:00 – 21:30, 1.5h)
**Purpose:** New concepts. Lecture + short notes. Tired brain → guided intake is fine.
**Do:**
- Watch the planned lecture(s) at 1.25–1.5× where comfortable (60–70 min)
- Write short notes in `notes/<subject>/` — definitions, formulas, one worked example, traps (20 min)
- Fill the daily log Reflection (5 min)
**Don't:**
- Attempt timed PYQs (you'll misread your own accuracy)
- Hop to a backup lecture series unless one specific concept didn't land
- Multi-task with social media

### Saturday block (09:00 – 13:00, 4h)
**Purpose:** Test under exam conditions, aptitude, consolidation.
**Structure:**
- 09:00 – 11:00 — **Timed topic test** on the week's subject (unseen PYQs 2000–2021, virtual calculator, negative marking). Phase 6+: full 3h mock 09:00–12:00.
- 11:00 – 12:00 — **General Aptitude** (rotation in `curriculum-aptitude.md`)
- 12:00 – 13:00 — Error analysis of the test, update error log, revise the week's short notes, compile Sunday review data

### Sunday — Rest + 30–45 min review
**Purpose:** Recovery. Plan next week.
**Do (Sun morning, ~30–45 min):**
- Write weekly review (`reviews/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD-to-YYYY-MM-DD.md`)
- Update all 8 trackers
- Set focus for next week
**Don't:**
- Solve new PYQs
- Watch lectures "to get ahead"
- Worry about catching up

## Phase-Specific Adjustments

### Phase 1 (Weeks 1-10) — Foundations
- Mornings: C output-tracing and DS PYQs — slower, 10–12 questions per session is fine
- Evenings: lectures; Discrete Maths lectures are dense — cap at 2 per evening
- Saturday: Week 1 is a full diagnostic paper instead of a topic test

### Phase 2 (Weeks 11-22) — Algorithms + Maths + Digital
- Mornings: 12–15 PYQs; maths weeks lean on virtual-calculator numericals
- Evenings: lectures + formula capture straight into `notes/formula-sheets/`
- Saturday: topic test; if taking GATE 2027 as practice, it replaces the Saturday (or Sunday) of whichever week your paper falls in — Weeks 21–23

### Phase 3 (Weeks 23-36) — Systems
- Mornings: 15 PYQs; COA and OS numericals are long — allow 5–6 min for 2-markers
- Wednesday PM from Week 28: 30-min interleaved quiz on an earlier subject (keeps Phase 1–2 alive), then lecture
- Saturday: topic test

### Phase 4 (Weeks 37-49) — Networks + Theory
- Mornings: 15–20 PYQs
- Wednesday PM: 30-min interleaved quiz continues
- Saturday: topic test

### Phase 5 (Weeks 50-60) — Revision
- Mornings: subject PYQ pass 2 (all 2000–2021 questions for the week's subject, year-wise)
- Evenings: re-read short notes, targeted re-watch of weak topics only, write the formula sheet
- Saturday: 2h full-subject test

### Phase 6 (Weeks 61-70) — Test Series
- Saturday: 09:00–12:00 full mock, 12:00–13:00 first-pass scoring
- Wednesday AM + PM: split mock (one paper across two 1.5h halves)
- Other slots: mock analysis + remediation (see phase file)

### Phase 7 (Weeks 71-72) — Final Sprint
- Mornings: formula sheets + error-log redo
- Evenings: short-notes rotation, 2 subjects per evening
- Saturday: last full mocks. Sleep schedule shifts to match exam slot timing.

## When Life Happens

**One-off disruption (sick day, family emergency):**
- Log it as "skipped" in the daily log
- Don't double-up the next day
- Move on

**Travel for work:**
- Evening lectures are the easiest to drop; mornings usually survive (phone + GO PDF)
- Aim for 50% normal volume during travel weeks
- Log honestly

**Heavy work weeks (release crunches, on-call):**
- Pre-week: tell the coach "this week will be 30% capacity"
- Stick to morning PYQ slots only — they protect retention of what you already covered
- Skip Saturday block guilt-free
- Resume normal next week; the subject extends by the capacity lost, rounded to the nearest half-week

**Burnout signals (act on, don't push through):**
- 5+ days of energy/focus <6/10 in daily logs
- Skipping evening sessions repeatedly
- Dreading the morning slot

If you see these, **take a full week off**, don't try to muscle through. The current subject extends 1 week. 72 weeks is long — a planned week off beats a crashed month.

## Calendar Recurring Blocks (set these once)

In Google Calendar / Outlook / whatever, create:

1. **Daily 08:30 – 10:00** — "GATE: PYQ AM" — Mon-Fri
2. **Daily 20:00 – 21:30** — "GATE: Lecture PM" — Mon-Fri
3. **Saturday 09:00 – 13:00** — "GATE: Test + GA Block"
4. **Sunday 09:30 – 10:15** — "GATE: Weekly Review"
5. **Last Sunday of month 09:00 – 10:30** — "GATE: Monthly Review" (replaces weekly review that day)
6. **One-off reminders:** 27 Sep 2026 (GATE 2027 registration, if practice attempt) · 1 Aug 2027 (check GATE 2028 brochure) · GATE 2028 registration close (add when announced)

Set notifications. Treat them as meetings. Don't reschedule lightly.

## What This Schedule Optimizes For

- **Sustainability** — 19h/week is feasible alongside a full-time senior engineer's job, for 17 months
- **Cognitive matching** — new concepts in the evening, recall under fresh focus the next morning (the 24-hour recall loop is the backbone)
- **Exam realism** — every Saturday is timed with negative marking and the virtual calculator, so the exam format is familiar long before Phase 6
- **One full rest day** — Sunday recovery is non-negotiable; you'll perform worse without it
- **Spaced repetition built-in** — error-log redos (daily), Saturday revision (weekly), interleaved quizzes (Phase 3+), revision rounds (Phase 5), formula-sheet sprint (Phase 7)
