# Phase 6 — Test Series (Weeks 61–70)

**Dates:** 2027-11-08 → 2028-01-16 (10 weeks)
**Budget:** ~190 hours (19h/week)
**Structure:** 2 mock papers per week — a full 3h mock on Saturday, a split mock on Wednesday — plus deep analysis and targeted remediation.
**Theme:** Convert knowledge into marks. From here, the unit of progress is the mock score and the marks lost per error type — not topics covered.

---

## Why This Phase Exists

Knowing the syllabus and scoring 75+ in 3 hours are different skills. The gap is question selection, time allocation, negative-marking discipline, NAT precision, and stamina. The only way to close it is repetition under real conditions with ruthless analysis.

The analysis matters more than the mock: **one mock + 3 hours of analysis beats three mocks with none.** That's why Monday is entirely analysis.

## The Standard Test-Series Week

| Slot | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| Mon AM | Re-attempt **every wrong and skipped** question from Saturday's mock, untimed, no key. Mark "could have got it" vs "genuinely didn't know" |
| Mon PM | Classify every lost mark: concept / formula / calculation / misread / time / guess. Log in `error-log.md`. Pick this week's two remediation subjects **A** and **B** (lowest % of their marks) |
| Tue AM | Remediation A: 15–20 PYQs on its weakest topics (pass-2 wrongs first) |
| Tue PM | Remediation A: targeted repair (notes + one lecture segment) |
| Wed AM | **Split mock, part 1:** first ~33 questions of an older paper, 90 min, timed |
| Wed PM | **Split mock, part 2:** remaining ~32 questions, 90 min, timed |
| Thu AM | Score the split mock (20 min) → Remediation B: 15–20 PYQs |
| Thu PM | Remediation B: targeted repair |
| Fri AM | GA: 10-question timed set (20 min) → 30 NATs on the virtual calculator, mixed subjects (precision drill) |
| Fri PM | **R4 revision rotation** (subject listed per week): read exam-version formula sheet + 15 random pass-2 PYQs |
| Sat 09:00–12:00 | **Full mock**, 3h, exact exam conditions, exam-day strategy from `exam-info.md` |
| Sat 12:00–13:00 | First-pass scoring: total, section-wise, negatives, attempted vs correct, time per section |
| Sun | Weekly review (+ monthly where marked). Mock trend in `progress.md` |

## Mock Rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Same start time every Saturday.** Once the admit card shows your session (forenoon / afternoon), shift Saturday mocks to that exact clock time.
2. **Virtual calculator only.** No phone, no physical calculator, no scratch Python.
3. **No pausing.** A mock interrupted for >10 min is logged as "compromised" and still analysed.
4. **Score honestly.** MSQ partial = 0. NAT outside the range = 0. Apply negatives.
5. **Every lost mark gets an error type** in `error-log.md` before Tuesday.
6. **Never check a score mid-week to "see if you improved".** Scores are reviewed on Sunday against the trend, not after each mock emotionally.

Before moving to Phase 7 (Mon 2028-01-17), confirm:

- [ ] Mock average ≥65 over M5–M11, with an upward trend

If the average is below 65: **Phase 7 does not get more mocks.** It gets more error-log redos and formula-sheet work on the two subjects losing the most marks. Scores rise from fixing leaks, not from volume.
