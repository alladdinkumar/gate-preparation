# General Aptitude Curriculum

**15 marks, fixed, common to every GATE paper.** At a PSU-level cut-off, GA is where rank is protected: toppers routinely score 13–15. It needs very little theory and a lot of steady exposure — so it gets one hour every Saturday for the whole 72 weeks, never a crash block.

**Time budget:** Saturday 11:00–12:00, Weeks 1–72 (~70h) + GA section in every mock.

**Primary resource:** GATE GA PYQs across all branches (GO PDF Volume 1). **Topic drills:** IndiaBix. Links in `resources.md`.

## Saturday Rotation (repeats every 9 weeks)

| Rotation week | Topic | Format (60 min) |
|---------------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | GA-4 Numerical computation | 10 min concept refresh → 25 GA PYQs / drills |
| 2 | GA-1 Grammar | 10 min rules → 25 questions |
| 3 | GA-5 Data interpretation | 30 min PYQs → 30 min calculator speed drill |
| 4 | GA-8 Logic + reasoning | 25 questions |
| 5 | GA-2 Vocabulary + GA-3 Reading / sequencing | 20 questions + 2 passages |
| 6 | GA-6 Mensuration + geometry | formula refresh → 20 questions |
| 7 | GA-7 Stats + probability + PnC | 20 questions |
| 8 | GA-9 Spatial aptitude | 20 questions (draw every one) |
| 9 | Mixed GA set — one full 10-question GA section, 25 min timed | score out of 15, log in `trackers/aptitude.md` |

Rotation index for week N (Weeks 1–49): `((N − 1) mod 9) + 1`. So Week 1 → rotation 1, Week 9 → rotation 9 (mixed set), Week 10 → rotation 1 again. Phases 1–4 run the rotation unchanged. Phase 5 uses the mixed set every other week. In Phases 6–7, GA is covered inside the full mocks, and the Saturday GA hour becomes mock analysis.

---

## Rules

- **Timed, always.** GA questions should average ≤2 min. Track time per question in the daily log.
- **Vocabulary is cumulative.** Every unknown word/idiom goes into `notes/aptitude/ga-02-vocabulary.md` with one example sentence. Re-read the list during every rotation-5 week.
- **Spatial questions are drawn on paper.** Never solve them mentally.
- **Target:** ≥12/15 on mixed sets by Week 36, ≥13/15 in the last 5 mocks.
